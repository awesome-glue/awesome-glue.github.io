Why Awesome Glue?

AWS Glue is powerful out of the box, but its ecosystem of reusable tools, container images, and best practices can be difficult to navigate. Awesome Glue brings these resources together.


We are an independent open-source community building libraries, preconfigured container images, and practical utilities that simplify AWS Glue development. From ETL to analytics workloads, Awesome Glue helps you get started faster, work more efficiently, and share knowledge with the wider community.
